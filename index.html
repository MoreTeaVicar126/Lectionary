Worship Apps

Free tools for those who plan and lead worship. Choose an app below, and save it to your device so that it works without a signal.

How to save an app for offline use
  • Tap Save for offline on the app you want, while you have a signal or Wi-Fi. When it says Available offline, it will open with no connection at all.
  • To give it its own icon, open the app, then on an iPhone or iPad tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  • Adding to the Home Screen is worth doing for Lectern: Safari can clear the saved data of websites you have not visited for a week, but not of apps on your Home Screen.
  • Anything you save or enter stays on the device you are using. It is not sent anywhere.
